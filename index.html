Frequently asked questions

When stock rises back above your threshold, Auto-PreOrder automatically deactivates pre-order mode and restores the normal "Add to Cart" button. Everything reverts cleanly, with no manual action required.

Auto-PreOrder works with all Shopify themes that support Online Store 2.0 (App Blocks). The storefront widget uses your theme's own CSS variables for colors and typography, so it always looks native. Legacy OS 1.0 themes are not supported.

One activation = one product entering pre-order mode. If the same product exits and re-enters pre-order mode in the same month, it counts as a new activation. The quota resets on the 1st of each month.

Uninstall the app from your Shopify admin. Shopify automatically stops billing at that point. You won't be charged for any unused portion of the month beyond Shopify's standard billing cycle.
